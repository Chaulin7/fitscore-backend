    Privacy & Anonymisation

    Two data flows: CV content is processed transiently — files are deleted from the server as soon as text extraction and scoring complete, and scoring runs inside CVsprings (no CV content is sent to any third-party AI service). Audit records are different: when you click Save to Audit (or have autosave on), the candidate name, file name, scores, your decision and notes, a 300-character job-description snippet and your reviewer email are stored in your organization's account until deleted or until your retention period expires. When Anonymise is enabled, contact details, links, address lines, early year numbers and stand-alone name lines are stripped from the CV text before scoring. Full details in the Privacy Policy.

    How Scores Are Calculated

    Overview

    Each CV's text is analysed by a deterministic matching algorithm that extracts structured signals and compares them against your job description across four dimensions. The same rules are applied identically to every CV in a batch. See the methodology page for full details.

    The Four Sub-scores

    • Keywords (KW) — Key terms from the job description found in the CV.
    • Skills (SK) — Technical and soft skills match between CV and role.
    • Experience (EX) — Seniority, years of experience, and domain relevance.
    • Education (ED) — Degree level, field of study, and certifications.

    Overall Score

    The four sub-scores are blended using the weights you set in the Scoring Weights panel. Bias toward skills or experience depending on the role.

    Score Bands

    80+ Strong Fit 50-79 Possible Fit 0-49 Weak Fit

    Scores are AI-generated and should be used as a decision aid alongside human judgement.
